# Design notes and provenance

Original implementation: dark graphite canvas, acid-lime accents, editorial typography, an animated orbital sculpture, and pastel generative project covers. Mobile layouts retain the visual identity without a heavy WebGL runtime. Animations respect reduced-motion preferences and pause when the tab is hidden.

Internet reference: [Codrops — The Journey of Creating a 3D Portfolio](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/01/21/the-journey-of-creating-a-3d-portfolio/) informed the balance of an interactive hero and simpler mobile content. No third-party portfolio code, copy, screenshots, or project claims were copied.

Imported asset: Space Grotesk from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk), self-hosted as `static/fonts/space-grotesk.ttf`. Its SIL Open Font License is retained in `static/fonts/OFL.txt`. No external font requests, analytics, or third-party JavaScript execute in visitors' browsers.

The three starter projects are explicitly labeled **concept studies**, not claims about Robin's completed work. Name defaults to Robin and location to Estonia; everything personal is editable in Studio → Profile. Contact links remain absent until supplied.
